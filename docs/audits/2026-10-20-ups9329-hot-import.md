## Repair

The outbound stationary HOT echo is correlated to the existing HOT placeholder
without changing its flight number, direction, tail, status, parking, fuel work,
timestamps or inventory. The inbound UPS9329 identity is untouched. Only the
normal Google correlation/snapshot metadata is persisted.

Recognition is deliberately bounded: same operation, one opposite-direction
arrival with HOT origin and no schedule, exact normalized flight, same physical
tail, one existing same-tail HOT placeholder, HOT outbound destination, and no
scheduled/actual departure time. Existing absent-time markers blank/None/`-`
are reused; `00:00` is a real time and does not qualify. Ambiguous or mismatched
identities fail safely. No missions are deleted, merged or duplicated.

Known existing normalized flight identity also takes precedence over stale row
positions when Google rows reorder. HOT placeholder tail digits no longer count
as a numeric UPS flight identity. Genuine flight changes and HOT-to-real-flight
conversion retain their existing service path. The PostgreSQL cross-direction
identity invariant is unchanged.

A new successful HOT correlation emits one identity-resolution log with the
operation, row, original Google flight, canonical mission ID/flight and tail.
Unchanged polls do not emit that log repeatedly. Integrity errors remain enabled.

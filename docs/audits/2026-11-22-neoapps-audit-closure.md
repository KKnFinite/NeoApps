## Closeout execution

The untouched starting revision failed 69 assertions in the Sektor routes and Ermac read-budget suites. The route fixtures omitted the physical schedule, checked retired navigation, or included a grant created by the login fixture in the subsequent GET's write count. The SQL recorder also matched vendor checks inside a larger authorization query and attributed the whole query to the vendor lookup. The repaired recorder measures the actual authority query separately. The suites pass 185 tests and 188 subtests, including read-only and authorization checks.

One application defect emerged from the traces. A read-only request could fetch the same gateway's weekly schedule for yesterday, today and the polling gate. The change loads the active weekday/Sort/code projection once per gateway per request. Separate requests and transactions recheck the schedule. The dedicated test covers multiple gateways, overnight eligibility and a committed schedule change. Operational writes, physical opening/closing rules and durable worker scheduling retain their existing paths.

### SubZero saved drafts at the Sort boundary

The original idle-screen fixture had no saved Outbound draft. On a signed-in page with a draft, opening Dispatch at 20:59 triggered a second, unsolicited operational GET on desktop Chromium and phone WebKit, one minute before the configured 21:00 opening. The request came directly from page initialization and bypassed the live controller. Dispatch now reconciles those rows on the controller's first eligible refresh. Ordinary rendering still restores local form fields. The same two browsers recorded **one automatic off-hours operational GET before and zero after**. They retained the draft, kept the historical selection idle across opening, and fetched the draft rows successfully when the operator reopened the eligible current view. This uses the existing polling interval and physical-window checks.

## Decisions and verification outstanding

1. **High · Identity assurance and independent recovery — BLOCKED BY APPROVAL.** Name the authorized administrator and an independent recovery custodian, approve the enrollment/recovery procedure and permit a controlled real-account drill. Restrict enrollment and recovery evidence to the private handoff.
2. **High · Production disaster recovery — BLOCKED BY APPROVAL.** Authorize one isolated restore of the existing snapshot before its expiry, a maximum spend/retention, named access, key custody and target recovery time/data loss. Workers and external integrations must stay disabled. A later cleanup of the restored resource requires separate approval.
3. **Medium · Infrastructure assurance — provider/owner evidence needed.** Supply redacted read-only records for effective runtime, migration and deploy privileges, network/TLS restrictions, credential scope/rotation, edge log retention/access and the deployed base-image SBOM. Any ensuing production policy change requires specific approval.
4. **Medium · Financial verification — comparable periods and provider data needed.** Obtain read-only period-level usage/invoices and historical service-initiated egress breakdown if retained. Match Nov 23 and 24 after both physical Sorts finish and compare a full unscheduled day. The scheduler's current bounded recovery interval remains the accepted implementation. Future work cannot reconstruct a missing historical destination meter.
